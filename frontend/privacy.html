Privacy Policy

This page explains how DocuMind handles information when you use the platform.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Information We Handle

DocuMind may process information that you provide while using the platform, including documents uploaded for analysis and questions submitted through the document intelligence interface.

2. Uploaded Documents

Documents uploaded to DocuMind are processed to extract text, create searchable representations and retrieve relevant information when you ask questions.

For a local development deployment, documents may be stored within the application's configured data directory.

3. Questions and Answers

Questions submitted to the document intelligence interface are processed to retrieve relevant document content and generate an answer.

Answers are intended to be based on the indexed document context available to the system.

4. Third-Party Services

DocuMind may use external services for functions such as AI answer generation or other application capabilities, depending on the deployment configuration.

Information sent to such services is subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

5. Data Security

DocuMind is designed with security considerations such as controlled API access, document processing boundaries and separation between application components.

Security controls may vary depending on whether DocuMind is running as a local development application or as a deployed service.

6. Data Retention

Data retention depends on the application's deployment and storage configuration.

In the current local development version, indexed documents can remain in the configured application storage until they are removed.

7. Your Choices

Depending on the deployment, users may choose which documents they upload and may remove locally stored documents through the application's storage system.

8. Policy Updates

This privacy policy may be updated as DocuMind evolves and new functionality is introduced. The updated version will be published on this page.

Important

This page is a product privacy-policy template for the DocuMind project and should be reviewed and adapted before being used as a legally binding policy for a production service.
