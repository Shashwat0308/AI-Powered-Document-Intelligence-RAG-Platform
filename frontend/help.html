Open the Document Intelligence workspace, upload a supported PDF, wait for it to be indexed and then ask questions about the document.

The current version of DocuMind supports PDF documents, including research papers, reports and technical documentation.

DocuMind extracts the document text, divides it into smaller chunks, generates embeddings and stores the resulting information in the vector index for retrieval.

DocuMind uses Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG). It first searches the indexed documents for relevant content and then uses that retrieved context to generate an answer.

Yes. You can ask questions naturally, such as "What is this paper about?" or "What methodology was used?"

Answers are generated using relevant content retrieved from your indexed documents. The interface can also show the document and page associated with retrieved sources.
