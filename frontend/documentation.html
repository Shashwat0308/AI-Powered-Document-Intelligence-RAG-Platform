Getting Started

DocuMind allows you to upload research papers, reports and other PDF documents and ask questions about their contents.

01

Open Document Intelligence

Open the Document Intelligence workspace from the landing page.

02

Upload a document

Select a PDF document and click Upload & Index Document.

03

Ask questions

Ask questions about the information contained in your indexed documents.

04

Review sources

DocuMind displays the document and page number used to generate the answer.

Supported Documents

The current version of DocuMind supports:

  • PDF documents
  • Research papers
  • Reports
  • Technical documentation
  • Text-heavy documents
